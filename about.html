About Traverse Ethiopia

Your trusted partner for authentic birding and cultural experiences in the heart of Ethiopia

Our Story

Traverse Ethiopia Tours was born from a deep love for Ethiopia's incredible biodiversity and rich cultural heritage. Founded in 2010 by a team of passionate local guides, we've spent over 15 years sharing the wonders of our homeland with travelers from around the world.

What started as a small group of birding enthusiasts has grown into a full-service tour company known for our expertise, professionalism, and genuine care for our guests. We believe in sustainable tourism that benefits local communities and preserves Ethiopia's natural and cultural treasures for future generations.
