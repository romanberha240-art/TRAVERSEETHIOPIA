Omo Valley Cultural

10-day cultural immersion in Ethiopia’s most fascinating tribal regions.

Day 1: Fly or drive to the Omo Valley.

Day 2: Visit the Hamer people and community cultural center.

Day 3: Mursi and Surma cultural experiences.

Day 4: Photography and historical storytelling walks.

Day 5: Scenic drives and landscape exploration.

Day 6: Market visit and local food tasting.

Day 7: Tribal interaction and traditional dance.

Day 8: Village homestay experience.

Day 9: Scenic return and rest.

Day 10: Depart from Addis.
