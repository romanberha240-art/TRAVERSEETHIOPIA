Danakil & Lalibela

14-day northern Ethiopia expedition of extremes and heritage.

Day 1: Arrive in Addis and rest.

Day 2: Travel to Bahir Dar and Lake Tana.

Day 3: Transfer north to Lalibela.

Day 4: Visit Lalibela churches.

Day 5: Heritage and cultural learning.

Day 6: Drive to the Danakil region.

Day 7: Desert adventure and salt flats.

Day 8: Explore volcanic landscapes.

Day 9: Return and scenic break.

Day 10: Addis city exploration.

Day 11: Free day / local market visit.

Day 12: Photography and community experience.

Day 13: Final preparation and farewell dinner.

Day 14: Departure.
