Bale Mountains Birding

7-day safari itinerary for bird and wildlife enthusiasts.

Day 1: Arrival in Addis and transfer to the highlands.

Day 2: Birding in Bale Mountains National Park.

Day 3: Endemic species tracking across the moorlands.

Day 4: Wildlife viewing and scenic hikes.

Day 5: Focused bird photography and trekking.

Day 6: Local community visit and cultural stop.

Day 7: Return to Addis and departure.
