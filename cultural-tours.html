Tribes of the Omo Valley

The Omo Valley is home to some of the most fascinating indigenous tribes in Africa, each with unique traditions and ways of life.

Hamer

Known for bull-jumping ceremonies and elaborate body adornment

Mursi

Famous for lip plates and scarification traditions

Konso

UNESCO World Heritage site with unique agricultural terraces

Dassanech

Pastoralists living along the Omo River
